## 1. Who we are

Wasli ("we", "us") is a service that lets you order from your car at a restaurant or cafeteria by chatting with the restaurant. It is run by [company legal name], [trade licence number], [registered address], [emirate], United Arab Emirates. For anything about your personal data, write to [privacy contact email].

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the Wasli customer app or website, why, who sees it, how long we keep it and what you can ask us to do. It does not cover the restaurants' own websites or what a restaurant does with an order after it receives it; each restaurant is responsible for that.

## 2. What we collect

| What | When | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Your name, and your email address or phone number | When you sign up | To create your account, send you a verification code and let you sign in |
| Your password | When you sign up | To protect your account. We store only a scrambled form (a hash) that nobody can read back |
| Verification codes | When you verify an email or phone | To prove the contact is yours. Codes are stored scrambled, expire after 10 minutes and are limited to five attempts |
| Your language (Arabic or English) | When you choose it | To write the chat in your language |
| Your messages in the chat | When you order | To understand and price your order and to show the conversation back to you |
| Your order | When you confirm it | The items, options, notes, prices and total, so the restaurant can prepare it and you can see its status |
| Your car details | When you answer the car question | The parking name and where your car is, the plate number, the model and the colour, so staff can bring the order to your car |
| Your note for the restaurant | If you write one | So the restaurant can read it |
| Your phone's position | Only when you allow your device to share it | To show the restaurant how far you are and where, so it can accept or reject the order. The position is sent with the order and never shown to other customers |
| What you say in a voice message | When you use the microphone | We turn the clip into text. **The audio is not stored**: it is processed in memory and dropped, and only the text of what was heard stays in the conversation |
| Sign-in sessions | When you sign in | A random token (stored scrambled) and the type of device or browser, so we can keep you signed in and sign you out |
| Technical data | Every request | Your IP address and basic request details, handled by our hosting providers and used for security, abuse prevention and rate limiting. We keep technical records of the assistant's use (which provider, how long it took, whether it worked) **without** your messages, voice or position |

We do not ask for payment card details: in this version, payment is settled between you and the restaurant [confirm before launch]. We do not use your data for advertising, we do not sell it and we do not build advertising profiles.

## 3. Why we use it, and on what basis

- **To provide the service you asked for** (create your account, run the chat, send the order to the restaurant, show you its answer). This is needed to perform the contract between you and us.
- **To keep the service safe** (verification codes, limits on abuse, blocking misuse, fixing errors). This is our legitimate interest in running a secure service.
- **With your consent** for your position and your microphone: your device asks you first, and you can say no or change your mind in your device settings. Without your position, you can still order, but the restaurant cannot see how far you are.
- **To meet legal duties** where the law requires us to keep or give information.

## 4. Who sees your data

- **The restaurant you order from** sees your name, order, note, car details and, if you shared it, your position, so it can serve you. Only that restaurant's staff see them. The restaurant then handles your order under its own responsibility.
- **Service providers who process data for us**, only as needed to run Wasli:

  | Provider | What for |
  | --- | --- |
  | Render | Hosting of the server |
  | Neon | The database |
  | Cloudflare | Hosting of the websites and storage of restaurant images |
  | Groq | Turning your voice into text and understanding your order. Receives the text of your message or the audio clip, the menu and the current order, and, for the car question, your answer; it never receives your position |
  | Google (Gemini) | The same understanding, only if we switch to it or compare it [confirm before launch] |
  | Resend | Sending verification emails [confirm before launch] |
  | [SMS provider] | Sending verification codes by text message [to be chosen] |

  These providers may only use your data to provide their service to us [confirm against each provider's terms].
- **Authorities**, when the law or a valid order requires it.
- **A buyer or successor**, if the business is sold or merged, under the same promises as this policy.

The assistant only reads what you write or say to understand your order. It does not write the replies you see or create orders: the server does, and only after you confirm.

## 7. How we protect it

Passwords, verification codes and sign-in tokens are stored only in scrambled form. Connections are encrypted. Each restaurant's staff can see only that restaurant's orders, and this separation is tested automatically. Your position is never written to our logs or sent to the assistant. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach puts your rights at risk we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

## 8. Your rights

You can ask us to:

- give you a copy of your personal data;
- correct data that is wrong;
- delete your data or close your account;
- stop or limit a use of your data, or object to it;
- withdraw a consent you gave (this does not affect what happened before);
- move your data to another service where that applies.

Write to [privacy contact email]. We may ask you to prove who you are, and we answer within [30 days]. If you are not satisfied, you may complain to the UAE Data Office or the authority that applies to you [counsel to confirm].

## 9. Children

Wasli is not for people under [18]. If you think a child gave us data, write to us and we will delete it.

## 10. What stays on your device

The app keeps your sign-in token and your language choice on your device so you stay signed in. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. You can sign out in the app, which cancels the token.

## 11. Changes

When we change this policy we post the new text with a new date, and tell you in the app if the change matters to you. Using Wasli after a change means you accept it, except where the law requires your consent.
